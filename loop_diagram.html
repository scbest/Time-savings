The 4-Part AI Loop

Most AI hacks are just Job + prompt. No trigger, no context, no boundary — that's why the workflow never sticks.
save · reference · share
01
Job
what you want done
The task itself. "Draft this." "Summarize that." "Prep my brief."
02
Trigger
when it runs
The schedule or signal. "Every weekday at 7pm." "When a new email lands."
03
Context
what it can see
Calendar, email, notes, tasks, messages, docs, analytics — whatever makes the job smarter.
04
Boundary
what it can't do without you
The guardrails. "Don't send or change anything without me."
