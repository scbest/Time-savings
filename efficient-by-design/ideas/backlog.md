# Idea Backlog & Format Mapping

## Principle: match format to substance

- Thin hack (~60–90s) → **Short**; combine several into a **bundle** long-form.
- Rich topic → **deep-dive** long-form + clip Shorts from it.
- Never pad a thin hack to 8 minutes — it kills retention and trips YouTube's
  inauthentic-content policy.

## The five starter ideas

| # | Idea | Natural length | Long-form strategy | Affiliate | Faceless method |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 30-min workout: moves, timing, gear, no-equipment hacks | deep ✅ | Deep-dive + Shorts | Strong (weights, bands, mat) | Stock exercise clips or own neck-down footage + text + AI voice |
| 2 | iPhone text-replacement shortcuts for kid/dr forms | ~90s | Bundle ("12 iPhone hacks") | Weak; high search/share | Screen recording |
| 3 | Claude / AI agents workflow | deep ✅✅ | Deep-dive + series | Strong (software, recurring) | Screen recording |
| 4 | Blanch & freeze veggies to save money + time | ~3–4 min | Deep-dive (per-veg times) or bundle | Medium (freezer bags, blancher) | Kitchen/hands B-roll + stock |
| 5 | CSA + mini delivery for time + health | ~2 min | Bundle ("grocery system") | Weak; brand-building | Stock + hands |

## Bundle candidates (thin hacks → one long-form)
- **"12 iPhone hacks every busy parent needs"** (includes #2)
- **"My whole grocery system: eat healthy + save time"** (includes #4 + #5)
- **"7 efficiency hacks that save me 5 hours a week"** (rotating mix)

## Deep-dive candidates (rich → long-form + series)
- **AI/Claude agents** (#3) — highest earning lane, monetizable earliest via software
  affiliates. Can produce many posts.
- **30-min workout** (#1) — best Amazon-affiliate driver.
