## 1. What this project is

A content-creation side hustle: a **faceless, AI-assisted "efficiency hacks" brand**
called **Efficient by Design**. The owner is a **B2B product manager and busy mom of
two** who loves efficiency across all areas of life (work, fitness, kids, kitchen,
tech/AI, meditation). The brand teaches people how to get more out of their time and
money.

**Owner's constraints and preferences (important):**
- **Does NOT want to use her own voice** → all narration is AI text-to-speech.
- Prefers **faceless** content (screen recordings, stock, hands — not her face).
- Wants a **small side income**, not high volume. Sustainability > hustle.
- Free tools only to start (no paid subscriptions, no watermarks).
- Gets bored on one narrow topic → the brand is deliberately **multi-topic**, unified
  by a single promise (see Brand).

## 3. Platform strategy

**YouTube is the hub and money engine. Everything else is a satellite.**

- **YouTube (primary):** evergreen search discovery (hacks get found for years —
  ideal for low posting volume), clickable affiliate links in descriptions, two
  income streams (ad revenue + affiliate), home for long-form deep-dives.
- **TikTok + Instagram Reels (reach):** cross-post the vertical Shorts for fast
  top-of-funnel growth; funnel viewers to YouTube + storefront. Same file, no extra work.
- **Pinterest (evergreen affiliate driver):** pin video thumbnails; efficiency/kitchen/
  organization hacks get heavy evergreen search there.
- **LinkedIn (bonus, one lane only):** for the AI/Claude-agents content — the owner is
  a B2B PM and that audience is on LinkedIn.

Rationale in one line: **search-driven + product-focused + low-volume + affiliate-
monetized** is the one creator profile where YouTube beats every alternative — and
that's exactly this profile.

## 4. Monetization plan (affiliate-first)

Ad revenue requires the YouTube Partner Program gate (1,000 subs + 10M Shorts views
in 90 days, OR 4,000 long-form watch hours) — slow. So **lead with affiliate**, which
pays before that gate:

- **Amazon Associates:** link the actual gear from each video (weights, bands, freezer
  bags, kitchen tools). RULES: approval is conditional; you must generate **3
  qualifying sales within 180 days** of applying or you're dropped (can reapply). No
  minimum subscribers required to be approved, but you need real traffic to hit the 3
  sales — so **apply only after ~6–8 weeks of steady views**, not on day one.
- **Amazon Influencer Program** (gives a storefront page): apply around ~1,000
  followers (no hard minimum, but it helps).
- **Software/AI affiliates:** for the AI/Claude-agent videos — higher payouts, often
  recurring, and **no sales deadline**, so this lane can earn earliest.
- Always add an **FTC disclosure** ("As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying
  purchases") and a privacy policy.

## 5. Content model

- **Shorts (≤3 min, vertical):** one hack each. Reach engine. Cross-post everywhere.
- **Long-form (regular video):** where the money is. Two legit ways to fill 8+ minutes
  WITHOUT padding (padding tanks retention and trips YouTube's policy):
  1. **Bundle** several small hacks into a themed video ("7 iPhone shortcuts that save
     an hour a week").
  2. **Deep-dive** one rich topic (AI agents, workout) with context → how → variations
     → mistakes → results.
- **The efficiency play:** one long-form bundle = 7 Shorts for free (clip each hack out).
- **Format facts:** A video is a *Short* only if ≤3 min AND vertical. Over 3 min = always
  a regular video. **No minimum length** for a regular video. **8 minutes** just unlocks
  mid-roll ads — not required. Retention beats length: a tight 5-min video that holds
  60% beats a padded 9-min that holds 20%.

## 6. Production workflow (all free, no watermark)

The key mental model: **video length comes from the VOICEOVER (script), not from one
long AI clip. Do NOT AI-generate the whole video.** Write a script, generate one
continuous AI voiceover (this sets runtime: ~140 words/min, so 10 min ≈ 1,400 words),
then lay a stream of short visuals over it (swap every 3–8s), add music + captions,
export.

| Step | Tool (free) |
| --- | --- |
| Script | ChatGPT / Claude |
| AI voice (not owner's voice) | ElevenLabs free tier (best), Microsoft Edge TTS (unlimited), or CapCut TTS |
| Visuals — priority order | 1) Own screen recordings / faceless footage (unique, policy-safe) → 2) Free stock (Pexels, Pixabay) → 3) AI images (Bing Image Creator, Adobe Firefly) → 4) occasional short AI video clips (Google Veo, Kling) as accents only |
| Graphics / diagrams | Canva (free) |
| Assemble + music + captions + export | **CapCut** (free, no watermark, 1080p 16:9 MP4) |
| Music | YouTube Audio Library, CapCut library, Pixabay Music |

CapCut is the workhorse: its editor exports with no watermark and includes TTS, music,
captions, and YouTube-format export. Always do one test export to confirm no watermark.

## 7. YouTube inauthentic-content policy (must stay compliant)

YouTube renamed "repetitious content" → "inauthentic content" (2025–2026) and actively
demonetizes mass-produced AI content. **Faceless and AI voice are allowed.** What gets
flagged: templated/repetitive videos, verbatim AI script over stock slideshow with no
original insight, and AI "experts" giving advice on sensitive topics (health, finance,
legal, politics). Stay safe by: original angle/insight per video, meaningful variation
between uploads, using own screen recordings/frameworks, and toggling the "altered or
synthetic content" disclosure in YouTube Studio on upload.
