# Platform & Monetization Strategy

## Why YouTube is the hub (for this specific brand)

Scored on the factors that matter for a **search-driven, product-focused, low-volume,
affiliate-monetized** creator:

| Factor | YouTube | TikTok | IG Reels | Pinterest | Blog/SEO |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Evergreen discovery (search) | Best | Weak | Weak | Strong | Strong (slow) |
| Affiliate mechanics | Best (desc links + storefront) | Link-in-bio only | Bio/stories | Links per pin | Best (in-text) |
| Two income streams (ads + affiliate) | Yes | Fund pays little | Inconsistent | No ad share | Ads need scale |
| Long-form home (deep-dives) | Yes | No | No | No | Text only |
| Buyer/solution intent | High | Low | Low | High | High |
| Rewards LOW volume | Yes (search compounds) | No (treadmill) | No | Yes | Yes |

**Decisive factor:** evergreen search. One YouTube hack video keeps earning for years;
TikTok/Reels posts die in days. That fits the "small volume, not a treadmill" goal.

**Platform roles:**
- **YouTube** = hub + money engine (long-form + Shorts).
- **TikTok + Instagram Reels** = reach engine (cross-post the same vertical Shorts).
- **Pinterest** = evergreen affiliate traffic (pin thumbnails).
- **LinkedIn** = bonus, only for the AI/Claude-agents lane.

## Monetization: affiliate-first

Ad revenue needs the YouTube Partner Program gate (1,000 subs + 10M Shorts views/90 days,
or 4,000 watch hours) — slow. Affiliate pays sooner.

### Amazon Associates — the real rules (2026)
- Approval is conditional; you must make **3 qualifying sales within 180 days** of
  applying, or the account is closed (you can reapply).
- **No minimum subscriber count** to be approved and drop links.
- **Do NOT apply on day one.** With no audience the 180-day clock runs out. Apply after
  ~6–8 weeks once you have steady traffic that can realistically drive 3 sales.
- **Amazon Influencer Program** (storefront page): apply ~1,000 followers.
- Add FTC disclosure + privacy policy.

### Earn earlier
- **Software / AI affiliate programs** (for the AI-agent content) often have higher,
  recurring payouts and **no sales deadline** — start this lane first.

## Launch plan

**Phase 0 — Prep (before posting):**
- Reserve `@efficientbydesign` on YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Pinterest.
- Same profile photo + bio everywhere.
- Batch 3–5 videos before launch (don't launch an empty channel).

**Phase 1 — Build reach (weeks 1–8):**
- 1 long-form/week + 2–4 Shorts (clipped from it). Cross-post Shorts to TikTok/IG.
- Goal: steady daily views / a few hundred subs.

**Phase 2 — Turn on money (~month 2–3):**
- Apply to Amazon Associates once traffic can convert.
- Add links + pinned comment + disclosure.
- At ~1k followers, apply to Amazon Influencer Program.

**Timing:** create → grow ~6–8 weeks → apply to Amazon → scale.
