## The 30-second version

- **Brand:** efficiency hacks across work, fitness, kids, kitchen, tech/AI, mindfulness.
- **Promise:** "Get the most out of every moment — save time, save money, live better."
- **Format:** faceless, AI voice (not the owner's), free tools, no watermark.
- **Platforms:** YouTube (hub) → TikTok / Instagram Reels / Pinterest (satellites).
- **Money:** affiliate-first (Amazon + software), ad revenue later.
