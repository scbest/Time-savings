# Production Toolkit & How-To (all free, no watermark)

## The core mental model

**Video length comes from the VOICEOVER, not from one long AI clip.**
Do NOT try to AI-generate a whole long video (those tools make short, capped clips).
Instead:

1. Write a script. Length math: ~140 words/min → **10 min ≈ 1,400 words**.
2. Generate ONE continuous AI voiceover of the script → this sets the runtime.
3. Lay a stream of short visuals over the voiceover, swapping every **3–8 seconds**.
4. Add a music bed (~20% volume, sits under the voice) + burned-in captions.
5. Export 1080p, 16:9, MP4, 30fps.
6. On upload, toggle "altered or synthetic content" disclosure.

To make a video **longer**: write **more script**. To keep it engaging: swap visuals
often and cut all padding.

## Tool stack

| Job | Tool (free) | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Script | ChatGPT / Claude | Ask for hook + retention beats |
| AI voice (not owner's) | ElevenLabs free (~10k chars/mo, most natural); Microsoft Edge TTS (unlimited); CapCut TTS | Pick a calm, warm voice on-brand |
| Stock video/photo | Pexels, Pixabay | Free, no watermark, commercial OK |
| AI images | Bing Image Creator (DALL·E 3, ~15/day, no watermark); Adobe Firefly (safest commercially, 25/mo) | For custom/consistent visuals |
| AI video clips | Google Veo (10/mo), Kling (free daily credits) | Accents only, 5–8s hero shots |
| Graphics/diagrams | Canva | Lists, frameworks, text cards |
| Screen recording | phone/computer built-in, or CapCut | For iPhone-hack + AI-agent videos |
| Assemble + music + captions + export | **CapCut** | No watermark; do one test export to confirm |
| Music | YouTube Audio Library, CapCut library, Pixabay Music | Cleared for YouTube |

## Visual sourcing priority

1. **Own screen recordings / faceless footage** (hands, neck-down, your real tools) —
   unique + exactly the "human input" the policy rewards.
2. **Free stock** (Pexels/Pixabay) — fill and B-roll.
3. **AI images** (Bing/Firefly) — polish and consistency.
4. **AI video clips** (Veo/Kling) — occasional accents only. Never the backbone.

## Faceless without the owner's voice

- Narration = AI TTS (never the owner's voice).
- On screen = stock, screen recordings, hands, or neck-down footage — never the face.
- Fitness is the one niche where showing a real body (no face) doing the moves boosts
  trust; kitchen and AI-agent videos are effortlessly faceless (hands + screen).

## Per-move visual pattern (e.g. workout)

For each timed instruction beat (~20–30s of video):
- One 5–15s demo clip, looped/trimmed (stock or own neck-down footage)
- On-screen text: move name + target ("Goblet Squat · 40 sec")
- Optional small timer graphic
- AI voice describes form + the no-equipment hack
- Music underneath
The "40 sec on / 20 off" is instruction for the viewer — NOT a real-time segment you
must perform on camera. (That would be a separate "follow-along" format.)
