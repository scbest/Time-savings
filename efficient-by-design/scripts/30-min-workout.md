**[2:00 — WARM-UP (4 min)]**
`[VISUAL: demo clips of each move; timer overlay]` `[TEXT: WARM-UP · 4 min]`
> Never skip this — it's how you avoid injury and work harder. Forty-five seconds each,
> no rest: marching in place with arm swings; arm circles forward then back; slow
> bodyweight squats; standing knee-to-elbow twists; leg swings holding something for
> balance. You should feel warm and slightly out of breath. That's the goal.

**[3:00 — CIRCUIT 1: LOWER + CORE (10 min)]**
`[VISUAL: move demos, timer]` `[TEXT: CIRCUIT 1 · 3 rounds · 40 on / 20 off]`
> Three rounds of these four moves. Forty seconds on, twenty rest:
> Goblet squats — hold one dumbbell at your chest. No weight? Load a backpack with books.
> Reverse lunges, alternating legs. Glute bridges — dumbbell on the hips or bodyweight.
> Plank — forearms down, core tight, hips level. Rest one minute, then round two. By
> round three your legs will be talking to you. That's how you know it's working.

**[5:00 — CIRCUIT 2: UPPER + CARDIO (10 min)]**
`[VISUAL: move demos]` `[TEXT: CIRCUIT 2 · 3 rounds]`
> Same structure — 40 on, 20 off, three rounds:
> Dumbbell rows — hinge at the hips, pull to your ribs. No dumbbells? Loop a band under
> your feet. Push-ups — on your knees or against a counter to scale. Overhead press with
> dumbbells or a band. Mountain climbers — your cardio spike, as fast as good form
> allows. Keep the rest short. The magic is in not stopping.

**[7:00 — FINISHER (2 min)]**
`[VISUAL: intense quick clips]` `[TEXT: FINISHER · 2 min]`
> Two minutes, all out, no rest. Thirty seconds each: jumping jacks, high knees, squat
> pulses, and a plank hold to finish. This is where 30 minutes starts to outperform an
> hour — you're spending energy, not time.

**[7:45 — COOLDOWN (2 min)]**
`[VISUAL: calm stretching, softer music]` `[TEXT: COOLDOWN]`
> Don't skip this either. Slow it down: quad stretch, hamstring stretch, a gentle
> child's pose, and three slow deep breaths. This is the meditation piece — thirty
> seconds of stillness to actually land in the moment after moving hard. Finish calm
> instead of frazzled.
