# Tapping to Care Less About What Others Think
*EFT tapping follow-along script — read aloud / teleprompter-ready*

> Paste into your teleprompter app, turn on voice-activated scroll, prop the phone at eye
> level, hit record, and just start talking. Read slowly and leave pauses where marked.

---

## INTRO (to camera, or voiceover — ~40 sec)

If you've ever replayed a conversation for hours wondering what someone thought of you, or
shrunk yourself so nobody could judge you — this one's for me too. Today we're tapping on
caring less about what other people think.

Tapping, or EFT, is just gently tapping on a few points on your face and body while we say
some things out loud. I'm not a doctor — this is just something that's genuinely helped me
feel lighter, and I want to walk you through it.

Let me show you the points first, then we'll do it together. You can tap along, or just
watch this round and join the next.

## SHOW THE POINTS (demonstrate — ~30 sec)

We tap gently, about seven times each, with two fingers. Eyebrow… side of the eye… under
the eye… under the nose… the chin crease… the collarbone… under the arm, about a hand below
the armpit… and the top of the head. That's the whole map. Don't worry about being precise —
close enough works.

---

## THE PRACTICE

### Setup — tap the side of your hand (the "karate chop" point) and say each line:

Even though I care so much about what other people think of me, I deeply and completely
accept myself.

Even though I'm tired of needing everyone's approval, I accept myself and how I feel.

Even though part of me feels unsafe when I'm judged, I'm open to letting a little of this go.

### Round 1 — naming it *(tap each point ~7 times)*

- **Eyebrow:** All this worry about what they think of me.
- **Side of eye:** I can feel it in my body when I imagine being judged.
- **Under eye:** This fear of getting it wrong in front of people.
- **Under nose:** I've carried this need for approval a long time.
- **Chin:** Part of me believes I'm safer if everyone approves.
- **Collarbone:** All this tension from managing what other people think.
- **Under arm:** This heaviness of trying to be liked by everyone.
- **Top of head:** I'm noticing how much space this takes up in me.

*(Pause. One slow breath.)*

### Round 2 — softening it

- **Eyebrow:** But I don't actually have to control what anyone thinks.
- **Side of eye:** Their opinion is happening in their mind — not mine.
- **Under eye:** I'm allowed to set a little of this down right now.
- **Under nose:** It's safe to loosen my grip on being approved of.
- **Chin:** I can care about people without needing them to approve of me.
- **Collarbone:** What they think of me is really none of my business.
- **Under arm:** I get to decide who I am.
- **Top of head:** I'm choosing to take my energy back.

*(Pause. One slow breath.)*

### Round 3 — the reframe

- **Eyebrow:** The people who matter don't need me to perform.
- **Side of eye:** I'd rather be real than be approved of.
- **Under eye:** I can be disliked and still be completely okay.
- **Under nose:** My worth was never up for a vote.
- **Chin:** I choose what I think of me.
- **Collarbone:** It feels lighter to let others have their opinions.
- **Under arm:** I'm allowed to take up space exactly as I am.
- **Top of head:** I care less about what others think — and more about what's true for me.

### Close *(hands down)*

Take a slow breath in… and let it go. Notice anything that feels even a little lighter than
when we started. That's it — that's the whole practice. Come back to it any time you feel
yourself shrinking for someone else. I'll see you in the next one.
