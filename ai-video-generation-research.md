# Free AI Video Generation for Faceless YouTube — Research & Recommended Workflow

_Last updated: 2026-07-16_

## Your requirements

1. Generate **longer-form** videos (not just 5–60 second clips)
2. **AI voice / text-to-speech** narration — you do **not** want to use your own voice
3. Ability to **pair music** with the video
4. **Free plan with no watermark** so you can experiment cheaply
5. Output **ready to post on YouTube** in the correct format
6. Goal: quickly test which video ideas actually get views

## Bottom line

**No single tool does all of this for free without a watermark on long-form video.** The tools that *feel* all-in-one (Invideo AI, Kapwing, Pictory, etc.) all either watermark their free exports, cap you at a few minutes, or both. True generative video (Veo, Kling, Seedance, Sora) is genuinely impressive but is expensive per second and caps at seconds-to-minutes on free tiers — it is not how you make a 5–20 minute video for free.

**The winning approach is a free combo built around CapCut.** CapCut's editor exports with **no watermark** on the free tier, includes **AI text-to-speech voices**, a **royalty-free music library**, auto-captions, and one-click **YouTube-format export (1080p 16:9)**. You feed it visuals + a script, and it does the voice, music, captions, and export for free.

## Free AI voice (TTS) options — since you don't want your own voice

- **Microsoft Edge TTS** (edge-tts.com or the `edge-tts` open-source tool) — **unlimited**, 300+ neural voices, 70+ languages, free for commercial use, no watermark. Best "free forever" pick.
- **ElevenLabs free tier** — most natural/expressive voices; ~10,000 characters/month free, watermark-free audio. Best quality for the money (which is $0).
- **CapCut built-in TTS** — convenient, decent quality, zero extra steps, no watermark.

## Free music (no copyright strikes on YouTube)

- **YouTube Audio Library** (studio.youtube.com) — free, cleared for YouTube, no attribution needed on many tracks.
- **CapCut's built-in music library** — free tracks cleared for use in CapCut exports.
- **Pixabay Music** — free, commercial-OK.

## Correct YouTube format settings

- **Long-form:** 1920×1080 (1080p), 16:9, MP4 (H.264), 24–30 fps.
- **Shorts:** 1080×1920, 9:16, MP4, under 3 minutes.
- CapCut exports directly to these presets.

## Suggested experiment plan (to find what gets views cheaply)

1. Pick 3–5 topic ideas.
2. Generate scripts with ChatGPT/Claude (free).
3. Fast-prototype each with **Invideo AI** free (watermarked is fine for testing *ideas* internally — do not post these).
4. For the ideas you like, rebuild the final in **CapCut** (no watermark) with Edge TTS / ElevenLabs voice + free music.
5. Post, watch retention + CTR, double down on what works.

## Honest caveats

- "Free, no watermark" claims on marketing pages are often for the *editor* but not the *AI-generation* feature of the same product — always do one test export before committing.
- Pure text-to-video (a whole long video generated from a prompt) at high quality is **not** free at length in 2026; the free path for long-form is **narration + stock/short-AI-clips assembled in CapCut**.
- Some tools shut down (e.g., Wondershare Virbo is ending service on 2026-07-30) — don't build a workflow on a single dependency.
